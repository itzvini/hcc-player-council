So… what is the Creature Club?

The Highrise Creature Club is built from two collectible NFT concepts: 11,111 one-of-a-kind creature avatars and the Highrise LAND NFTs we brought into the same club. Hold either one and you're a member, with perks built into Highrise.

The basics

So, what's an NFT?

Think of an NFT as a one-of-a-kind digital collectible that's genuinely yours, recorded on the blockchain, not just sitting inside an app. Your Creature is yours to keep, wear, sell, or gift, and no one can copy or take it.

The club

What's the Creature Club?

It's 11,111 unique creature avatars, plus the Highrise LAND parcels folded into the same family. Members unlock wearable looks, daily Creature Coins, exclusive drops, and members-only spaces built into Highrise.

Gen 2 is on the way: every LAND will receive its own Creature, and every Gen 1 Creature its own pet, so Creatures and LAND end up equal in perks.

Membership

How do I become a member?

Just own a Creature or a LAND NFT. That's the whole requirement. Either one grants full Creature Club status: daily Creature Coins, exclusive drops, and the private community spaces.

Your Discord role is automatic. Once your NFT sits in a wallet linked to your Highrise account, your roles update on their own, usually within an hour. Nothing to claim on your side.

How HCC got here

Where the club has been, and where it's headed.

2021 · THE MINT

11,111 creatures are born

HCC launches as Highrise's first collectible avatar project: computer-generated creatures, each with a wearable set of items and a promise of perks for holders.

2022 · LAND ARRIVES

A second collectible: Highrise LAND

LAND launches as virtual parcels of the Highrise universe, in Standard and Premium tiers, with daily Land Tokens, room capacity boosts, an exclusive shop, and slime pets. The original dream: custom, monetizable corners of Highrise you could run yourself.

2022–2024 · THE WOBBLE

Big ideas, uneven follow-through

Lots of ambition across both projects, but consistency, communication and follow-through didn't meet the standard holders deserved. We heard the frustration.

2023 · LAND JOINS THE CLUB

One family, not two

We wound down the original "custom monetizable Highrise" plan for LAND and brought LAND owners into Creature Club benefits. We converted Land Tokens to Creature Coins, pets stayed with their owners, Premium LAND mirrored higher-tier Creature benefits at 3×, and a compensation path covered owners who didn't want the new direction.

2024 · THE MERGE SHIPS

LAND benefits become real HCC benefits

Regular updates confirmed the practical merge: Discord access opened to LAND owners, Highrise+ eligibility and Creature Coins applied to eligible players, exclusive LAND and Creature giveaways continued, and Gen 2 / shop work moved forward under one HCC update rhythm.

DEC 2025 · THE DELIVERY RESET

Back to visible follow-through

A dedicated artist put permanently on finishing Gen 1 & Gen 2. Updates every two weeks. Monthly shop refreshes. Quarterly drops. A clear commitment to communication, delivery, and rebuilding trust.

Quick answers

Questions you might have

I own LAND, not a Creature. Am I in the club?

Yes. LAND owners are full Creature Club members. Your Land Tokens became Creature Coins, you earn daily Coins (Premium plots at 3×), and you take part in club drops and giveaways. Your plot and your slime pet stay exactly as they are.

Is my creature's ownership safe?

Yes. Plans to migrate creatures off the blockchain have been canceled following Steam approval. If that ever needs to change, we'll tell you early and clearly. No surprises.

How much does a Creature cost, and what if I can't afford one yet?

Prices are set by holders, so they range widely: from a few hundred dollars to several thousand, depending on rarity and traits. For live numbers, the Market page shows the current floor and real sale history. Can't buy in yet? Keep an eye on the Highrise Discord. There are regular giveaways and contests where you can win one for free.

I bought a Creature but don't see my role or perks yet. What gives?

First, make sure the wallet holding it is linked to your Highrise account. That's what connects your NFTs to the club. Once it's linked, your Discord roles and perks sync on their own, usually within an hour. If it's been longer, double-check you linked the right wallet, then ask in the public Highrise Discord.

Where do I buy and sell, and how do I spot a fake?

Start here. The club's own Marketplace lets you browse, buy, sell and transfer straight from your own wallet, in ETH or USDC, with no marketplace fee from us. If you'd rather trade elsewhere, here's where each collection actually lives, so you can check any listing you come across.

  • 🐉 Gen 1 Creatures live on Immutable zkEVM. Off-site, the one official marketplace is Token Trove.
  • 🗺️ LAND and its slime pet live on Ethereum mainnet. Off-site, the one official collection is Highrise LAND on OpenSea.
  • 🧟 Gen 2 isn't out. It's still in production, so it doesn't trade anywhere yet. Anything sold as a Gen 2 creature or pet today is fake, without exception.

The fake to know: Gen 1 Creatures are not on OpenSea. Every "Highrise Creature" or "Highrise Creature Club" collection you see there is a scam. Reach any marketplace through the official links, never a DM, and read Stay safe before your first trade.

Quick answers

Questions you might have

Is this a DAO? Do I own part of the company now?

No. The Council is advisory: a sounding board on creative and roadmap decisions. Pocket Worlds remains the executor and keeps final say on the business, the economy and the brand.

Will big holders just take over?

No. It's one holder, one vote in every seat, never weighted by how many Creature or LAND assets you own. How many you hold only affects which seat you can run for, never how much your vote counts. We reserve seats for different holder sizes so everyone gets a voice. Four of the seven seats are elected directly by holders; three are appointed for continuity and competence. We'd rather say that split openly than pretend it's purely democratic.

What about Gen 1 and Gen 2?

A dedicated artist is on finishing them, and the Council helps decide which creatures and variants make each Gen 2 set, picking from the options the art team can build.

What does the Council start with?

It starts advisory-leaning, and its "Decides" power grows release by release as it earns trust, not everything on day one.

Your questions

Asked since the last update

These came out of Discord in the days after update #6. If yours isn't here, ask it in creature-chat and it'll end up on this page.

I own a Creature and no LAND. Do I get anything?

Yes. Every Gen 1 Creature is owed a Gen 2 pet slime, whether or not you hold a plot. Own three Creatures and you're owed three pets.

I own LAND. Am I getting a slime or a creature?

A creature. Plots already have their slime pets, so the Gen 2 side of LAND is a creature attached to the plot. Premium plots get exclusive ones.

Is a new NFT landing in my wallet?

No. Nothing new arrives on its own. The Gen 2 piece attaches to the NFT you already hold, so you end up with one asset carrying both halves rather than two assets side by side.

Can I sell the pet on its own, or keep it and sell the Creature?

No. The two stay together, and that was always the intent: bundling them is what makes your Gen 1 worth more rather than competing with a shinier Gen 2. Vini hasn't ruled out building a way to trade an attached Gen 2 later on, but that's a conversation for after Gen 2 ships, not a plan today.

Do I choose mine, or is it random?

Plan on random. The team will try to fit a pet to the Creature it lands on, so a dark Creature may well end up with something that suits it, but nothing about that is promised and you won't be picking from a menu.

Will I earn more Creature Coins once I have both?

No. Coins pay out per Creature and per LAND plot you hold, and Gen 2 doesn't add one to your count. Buy another NFT and you earn more; getting your Gen 2 half doesn't change your daily rate.

Do rarer NFTs get rarer Gen 2?

Partly, yes. Every Premium LAND plot and every Legendary Creature gets rarer, more exclusive Gen 2 assets. Below those two tiers nothing is settled, so what you've read about holographic Creatures or the ten 1-of-1s pulling special pets is still holder guesswork. When the rest is decided you'll read it here first.

Is the whole thing sushi?

No. Sushi is the first slime theme we've shown, not the whole thing. Dessert is being drawn now, then Nature, Celestial and Elemental, and both the count and the themes themselves can still change as the work lands. The creature sets for LAND are separate again: vampires, werewolves, demons, ghosts, slimes and the Yogurt Zombies.

Why do they look like concepts from years ago?

Because they are, and that's deliberate. The lineup comes straight from the preview shared back then, cleaned up rather than redesigned, because holders asked for the originals before anything new. Slimes were the first pets Highrise ever had and they're simple by nature, so the variety comes from five themed sets instead of five hundred shapes. Plenty of you have said they don't measure up to what the game ships now, and that's fair feedback, honestly given. It's read, and it's being weighed against keeping faith with the original concepts.

When do I get it?

There's no date, and we'd rather say that than invent one. What you can hold us to is a progress update every two weeks and a public board per set, so you can watch the work move instead of taking our word for it.

Won't bundling wreck the market if demand drops?

Straight answer from Vini: if buyers won't pay the asking price, the asset doesn't move, and a seller who wants out lowers their price. There's no plan to step into the market and prop it up. The plan is to keep shipping releases and perks so the collection stays worth wanting. Gen 2 is a perk that adds to HCC, not a promise about price.

Is HCC just Gen 2 now?

No. Drops and Creature Store releases carry on alongside it, and pet giveaways plus slime accessory grabs and store items are likely once the pets land. Gen 2 is the debt being paid. It isn't the whole club.

The scam to know: no Gen 2 pet or creature exists yet, anywhere. Any listing, DM or "early Gen 2 airdrop" offering one is a theft attempt. Read Stay safe before you click anything, and reach every marketplace through the official links.

1
Setup

Find Token Trove & link your wallet

Get to the official marketplace and connect MetaMask so you can see your balances and creatures.

  1. Open the Highrise Discord and go to the official links channel. Find the link to buy Highrise creatures. It takes you straight to Token Trove.
  2. Have your MetaMask wallet ready (extension or mobile app) and make sure you're logged in.
  3. On Token Trove, click Connect Wallet. Choose Immutable zkEVM, then pick MetaMask.
  4. Approve the connection in MetaMask. Token Trove now shows your balances (ETH, IMX, USDC) and your creatures under My NFTs.
2
Funding

Bridge ETH & IMX to Immutable

Bridging moves your crypto from Ethereum mainnet to the Immutable zkEVM network where creatures live. Before bridging, you'll need a small amount of $IMX: every action on Immutable requires it as a network fee. Balances can take 20–40 minutes to appear.

  1. Before anything else, swap a little $ETH to $IMX in MetaMask. $IMX is the fee currency for every action on Immutable zkEVM (buying, selling, transferring). Around 0.0025 $ETH worth is plenty to start. Do this swap before bridging.
  2. On Token Trove, confirm you're connected and logged in, and that you can see the Bridge button. Click Bridge.
  3. Bridge IMX first. Pick IMX as the token, enter an amount (e.g. 20 IMX), check the small fee, and confirm.
  4. Your wallet switches to Ethereum mainnet automatically. Confirm the two MetaMask pop-ups: the network fee, then the transfer itself.
  5. Now bridge your ETH the same way. This is what you'll buy creatures with. Pick ETH, enter an amount (e.g. 0.025), and confirm the single request.
  6. Wait roughly 20–40 minutes. When it's done, your ETH and IMX balances appear on Token Trove, ready to use.

Remember: $ETH buys creatures, $IMX pays the gas. You need a little $IMX for anything you do on Immutable: buying, selling, or transferring.

3
Trading

Buy & sell creatures

With funds bridged over, you're ready to shop the collection or list one of your own.

  1. Browse the collection and pick the creature you want. Before buying, switch your wallet to the Immutable zkEVM network: open MetaMask, select Immutable zkEVM, then refresh the page.
  2. Click Buy Now. Review the small network fee (this is what IMX is for) and click Confirm.
  3. Once the blockchain confirms, the creature is yours. Refresh your balances and check My NFTs to see it.
  4. To sell, open one of your creatures and click List Now. Set your price and confirm the signature request in MetaMask.
  5. Your creature is now listed. Anyone can buy it directly. You don't have to do anything else until it sells.

When a creature sells, a 5% creator royalty is built into the smart contract and comes out of the sale price automatically. Worth factoring into the price you set.

4
Moving

Transfer a creature to another wallet

Send a creature straight to another wallet, handy for moving between your own wallets or gifting.

  1. Open the creature you want to send and click the Transfer button, the little paper-airplane icon.
  2. Paste the destination wallet address. Double-check it: transfers can't be undone.
  3. Review the small gas fee and click Confirm. The Immutable zkEVM network confirms the transfer.
  4. After a few seconds, refresh My NFTs. The creature leaves your wallet and lands in the new one. A full page refresh helps if it lingers.

Always paste and verify the address yourself. There's no undo, and no one can reverse a transfer to the wrong wallet.

In-game

LAND

Mint LAND you bought with Deeds

Bought a parcel during an in-app LAND Deeds sale? It lives inside Highrise until you mint it onto the blockchain. Minting is what lets you sell, transfer, or trade it on OpenSea.

  1. Go to highrise.game and log in with the Highrise account that holds your plot. Make sure your MetaMask wallet is ready.
  2. Click your avatar in the top-right corner, then open Inventory. Your plot (or plots) shows up there.
  3. Click the specific plot you want to put on-chain, then press Withdraw.
  4. MetaMask pops up. Confirm and Sign the transaction. Keep a little ETH in your wallet to cover the gas fee.
  5. Once it confirms, your parcel is minted onto the blockchain and ready to trade, sell, or transfer on OpenSea.

Until you mint it, the plot stays tied to the Highrise account it was bought with. It can't move between accounts or wallets until it's on-chain.

Cashing out your USDT refund?

Club refunds are paid in USDT on Ethereum, sent to the wallet that returned the assets. That's good news: USDT is a dollar coin, so 210 USDT is worth about 210 dollars today, next week and next month. Your refund doesn't ride the crypto market while you decide, there's no clock on selling it, and every route above takes it at close to face value. Here's the trip, step by step.

  1. Switch MetaMask to Ethereum. That's the network refunds arrive on, and yours shows as a USDT balance under your tokens, usually within five business days of your transfer. Got USDT from somewhere else instead? Check the network picker first: coins on Tron, Solana or another network take a different road, and these steps fit Ethereum only.
  2. Ignore copycat tokens. Scammers watch these payouts and send a fake coin also named "USDT" minutes after the real one, so a block explorer may show your refund twice. The balance MetaMask shows on its own is the real one. They strike again the moment you send: a fake token fakes a second send from your wallet to an address that starts and ends like the real one. Never open a website a strange token points you to, never swap or approve a coin you didn't expect, and never copy an address from your history.
  3. Keep a little ETH in the same wallet. Moving USDT costs a small fee paid in ETH, so with zero ETH the coins can't move at all. A few dollars' worth is plenty.
  4. The easy route is the same sell page as for ETH: portfolio.metamask.io/sell, on a computer. Pick USDT as the coin to sell and compare the offers. It quoted mainnet USDT when we checked on 30 August 2026, at about 3% under face value.
  5. The cheap route is an exchange, and all three above take USDT. On the deposit screen pick USDT first, then the network, and it must say Ethereum or ERC-20 to match your wallet. Kraken alone offers eighteen networks for USDT, which is exactly how picking by eye loses the coins. Send a small test before the rest, and paste the address fresh from the exchange for the rest. Your history is where the fakes live.
  6. Once it lands, sell the USDT for your currency (near 1:1, since it's a dollar coin) and withdraw to your bank the way the cards above describe.

Know the playbook

Common scam tactics

Tap any card for how the trick works, the red flags, and exactly what to do. These are the ones actually hitting Highrise holders.

📥 Fake airdropped NFTs A "free" item you never bought lands in your wallet
How it works

Scammers send an unsolicited NFT (often a lookalike like a fake "Highrise Estate") straight to your wallet. Its image, name or description carries a link to "claim", "unlock" or "sell" it. That link leads to a drainer site that asks you to connect and sign, and the signature hands over your real assets. Just receiving it is harmless. Interacting with it is the trap.

Red flags
  • An item appears that you never bought or claimed.
  • Its details push you to visit a site to "claim" or "redeem" value.
What to do
  • Don't click anything on it. Don't try to sell, transfer or "claim" it.
  • Hide it in your wallet or marketplace and carry on. It can't hurt you sitting there.
✍️ Wallet-drainer signatures One "harmless" approval hands over everything
How it works

A site tells you to sign to "connect", "verify" or "claim". The request is actually a token approval or a blanket "set approval for all" that lets the scammer move your Creatures or funds whenever they like. It feels routine, so people click through without reading.

Red flags
  • A signature request you didn't start, or that mentions "approve", "permit" or "set approval for all".
  • The site rushes you or says signing is "just to log in".
What to do
  • Read every wallet popup. If you can't explain what it approves, reject it.
  • Review and revoke old approvals now and then with a tool like revoke.cash.
🎧 Fake support & impostor DMs "Staff" messages you first to help
How it works

Someone copies a team member's name and avatar and DMs you offering to "fix" a problem or "verify" your wallet. They send you to a phishing site or a fake ticket bot. Real staff and support never message you first.

Red flags
  • An unprompted DM claiming to be staff, support or a "helper".
  • Pressure to act fast, move to another app, or open a link.
What to do
  • Assume any first-contact DM is a scam. Don't reply, don't click.
  • Ask your question in the public Highrise Discord instead.
🔑 Seed phrase & "wallet validation" A form asks for your 12 words
How it works

A site or DM claims your wallet needs to be "validated", "synced", "migrated" or "restored", then shows a box to type your 12-word seed phrase. Anyone who gets those words owns your wallet forever. No real app or person ever needs them.

Red flags
  • Any request to enter, confirm or "back up" your seed phrase online.
  • Words like validate, sync, migrate or restore attached to your wallet.
What to do
  • Never type your seed phrase anywhere. Close the page immediately.
  • Keep it offline only. MetaMask never asks for it after setup.
🛒 Fake marketplaces & lookalike sites A convincing clone of the real thing
How it works

Scammers spin up a fake "Highrise Creature" collection on OpenSea, or a phishing clone of Token Trove at a near-identical web address. You connect your wallet expecting the real site and sign a drainer request. Gen 1 Creatures only trade on Token Trove. Any Gen 1 "Highrise Creature" collection on OpenSea is fake.

Red flags
  • A web address with an extra letter, dash or odd ending you don't recognize.
  • A "Highrise Creature" Gen 1 listing anywhere but Token Trove.
What to do
  • Reach every marketplace through the official links in the Highrise Discord.
  • Check the exact address before connecting. See the Links tab for the real ones.
🎁 Fake giveaways & free mints "You won" something you never entered
How it works

A post or DM says you won a prize, a whitelist spot, or a limited free mint. To "claim" it you connect your wallet and sign. The claim is a drainer, or the "small gas fee" is the theft itself. Urgency and a countdown are the bait.

Red flags
  • A win or free mint you never signed up for, with a ticking timer.
  • "Just connect and sign to claim" or "pay a small fee to release it".
What to do
  • Treat every surprise prize as fake until confirmed in an official channel.
  • Real HCC drops come through official Highrise channels, never a random DM.
💬 Discord account & bot compromise A hacked friend or fake "verify" bot
How it works

A hacked friend's account sends you a link, or a fake bot tells you to "re-verify" by scanning a QR code or authorizing an app. Scanning a Discord login QR from a stranger hands them your account. Malicious links can also install software that reads your wallet.

Red flags
  • A "verify", "re-verify" or QR-scan prompt from a DM or unknown bot.
  • A friend suddenly sharing a link, a giveaway, or a "free Nitro" offer.
What to do
  • Never scan a login QR you didn't generate yourself. Turn on 2FA.
  • If a friend sends something odd, confirm with them another way first.
📋 Address poisoning A lookalike address in your history
How it works

Scammers send tiny transactions from an address that starts and ends with the same characters as one you use. Later, you copy the address from your history to send funds, grab the lookalike by mistake, and the money goes to them. Transfers can't be reversed.

Red flags
  • Tiny unexpected transfers from an address that looks familiar.
  • Relying on your transaction history to copy a "known" address.
What to do
  • Check the full address, not just the first and last few characters.
  • Send a tiny test amount first, or use a saved, verified address book.
💵 Fake USDT refund tokens Your refund seems to arrive twice
How it works

The club's refund program pays in real USDT on Ethereum. Scammers watch those payouts on-chain and, minutes later, send the same wallets a worthless copy: a token with the same name and the same amount. On a block explorer the two look identical, so it reads like a second refund. The copy exists to make you touch it. Trying to sell, swap or "claim" it leads to a site whose approval hands your wallet over. We've watched this shadow real refund payouts, about fifteen minutes behind each one.

There's a second act. The moment you send real USDT anywhere, even a one-dollar test to an exchange, a fake token fakes a send from your wallet to an address that starts and ends like the real one. Your history now shows two sends. Copy the wrong one for the rest and it's gone for good. We watched this hit a refunded member twelve seconds after their test.

Red flags
  • Your refund shows twice on a block explorer, but MetaMask lists it once.
  • A "USDT" balance pushes you toward a website, or a swap you didn't start.
  • Right after you send USDT, a second send you never made appears in your history, to an address that starts and ends like the real one.
What to do
  • Trust the balance MetaMask shows on its own. That's the real USDT, and the double is the fake.
  • Don't sell, swap or approve the copy. It can't hurt you sitting there. Then cash out the real one with the USDT steps in the Marketplace guide.
  • When you send the rest, don't copy the address from your history. Paste it fresh from the exchange's deposit screen and check the whole thing, middle included.

Anatomy of a scam

How an airdrop empties a wallet

The exact chain of events behind the fake-NFT trick, so you can spot where to stop it. The safe move is to break the chain at step one.

  1. 1

    A "gift" arrives

    An NFT you never bought shows up in your wallet. It's designed to look valuable and to make you curious.

  2. 2

    It lures you to a site

    Its name or description carries a link to "claim", "sell" or "unlock" it. The link opens a drainer dressed up as a real marketplace.

  3. 3

    You connect and sign

    The site asks you to connect and approve a request. It looks routine, but the signature grants access to your real assets.

  4. 4

    The wallet is drained

    With that approval, the scammer moves out your Creatures and funds. On-chain transfers are final, so there's no undo.

Break the chain at step one: never interact with an item you didn't buy. Receiving it is harmless. Clicking is the trap.

    If you've been hit

    Act now, in this order

    Clicked a bad link or signed something wrong? Move fast. Speed matters far more than embarrassment, and everyone has a close call eventually.

    1. 1

      Disconnect and go offline from it

      Close the site, disconnect your wallet from it, and stop signing anything. Don't approve one more request "to fix it".

    2. 2

      Move your assets to a safe wallet

      If you still have access, send your Creatures, LAND and funds to a fresh wallet whose seed phrase has never touched a website. Do the most valuable first.

    3. 3

      Revoke the approvals

      From the compromised wallet, revoke token and collection approvals with a tool like revoke.cash. This cuts off the access the signature gave away.

    4. 4

      Secure your accounts

      Change passwords and turn on 2FA for Discord and your email. If a device feels compromised, run a malware scan before you sign anything again.

    5. 5

      Report it and warn others

      Tell the team in the public Highrise Discord with what happened and any links or addresses involved. Your report protects the next holder.

    A compromised seed phrase can't be made safe again. Once assets are on a fresh wallet, treat the old one as burned and stop using it.

    Terms of Participation

    Acceptance. Voters tick "I agree to the Terms" after logging in with Discord, before their first ballot; candidates explicitly accept at nomination submission. Acceptance is recorded with your Highrise Person ID, Discord ID, and a timestamp.

    1. Nature of the Council

    The Council is advisory only; Pocket Worlds keeps final say on business, economy, brand and legal. A seat is not ownership, equity, a security, a token right, employment, or any financial entitlement, and is not a DAO membership.

    2. Volunteer / no compensation

    Participation is voluntary and unpaid. There is no employment, contractor, or partnership relationship. Any perks are gifts we can change or withdraw. They're never payment or a right.

    3. Eligibility

    Holdings = combined Creatures + LAND at the published snapshot.

    • Voters: held at least 1 HCC NFT continuously for the 3 months ending at the snapshot.
    • Candidates: meet the bracket asset count at the candidacy snapshot and the 3-month continuous-holding bar; self-nominate in the window by answering the application's open questions.
    • Eligibility is assessed against your Highrise account's current primary wallet (holdings across multiple wallets are not aggregated). Snapshots are final.

    4. Voting

    One vote per elected seat (4 seats, plurality). You authenticate by logging in with Discord, which we match to your Highrise account to confirm eligibility. No wallet connection or signing is required, and we never move or spend your assets. Voting is per Highrise account (one person, one vote per seat). Votes are final once cast and cannot be changed. Vote privacy: individual votes are not published. Only per-seat tallies are. Each voter gets a private, verifiable receipt to confirm their vote was recorded and counted; the receipt is yours alone and does not reveal your choice to others.

    5. Candidacy: submission, publication & review

    By self-nominating you grant Pocket Worlds a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to publish, display, translate, and process your answers to the application's open questions for the election and the Voting Advice Application (VAA). The tool maps each written answer to a position, but your confirmed positions are authoritative: you review and edit them before voting opens, and your confirmed positions are published. You are responsible for the accuracy of your submission. The full VAA methodology will be published. All candidacies are subject to review of account background and any community reports; Pocket Worlds may decline or remove a candidacy at its discretion, and the reasons will be disclosed privately to the affected candidate.

    6. Terms of service & seats

    Elected terms run 6 months, max two consecutive for elected seats. Seats are personal and non-transferable: never sold, lent, or assigned. You may resign at any time. A seat left vacant mid-term stays empty until the next election. Vacancies are not backfilled during a term.

    7. Transparency / no confidentiality

    The Council operates openly: members are under no confidentiality obligation and may discuss and share Council materials publicly. Accordingly, Pocket Worlds will share with the Council only materials it is comfortable being made public. Nothing shared should be assumed secret or treated as a leak.

    8. Code of conduct

    No harassment, hate, threats, or doxxing. No vote-buying, bribery, or exchanging anything of value for votes. No impersonation, fraud, or material misrepresentation. No sybil/multi-account voting and no snapshot manipulation. Disclose conflicts of interest. Follow Highrise/HCC community guidelines and applicable law.

    9. Integrity & enforcement

    One eligible account, one ballot per seat. Pocket Worlds may void votes, disqualify candidates, remove members, or bar future participation for breach, at its reasonable discretion, with reasons published where practical.

    10. Data & privacy

    To authenticate and check eligibility we process your Discord ID, Highrise ID, Person ID, linked wallet address(es) and wallet-change history (via Discord OAuth and Highrise's internal API), plus the holdings snapshot, vote records (kept private), candidate answers (public), and voter answers (private; used to compute your own affinity, not published per-user). Candidate answers are sent to Anthropic (Claude) so the tool can map each into a position the candidate confirms. On-chain/wallet data is inherently public.
